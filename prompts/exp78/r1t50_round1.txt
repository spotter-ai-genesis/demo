SESSION
You are analysing experiment 78 at the Weston phenotyping facility: a cadmium (Cd)
dose series on 6 Brassica napus (oilseed rape) accessions at 0, 25, 50, 100, 250
and 500 mg kg-1, imaged over 28 days in six modalities.

This is a 3-round investigation. Each round gives you the results of the tool
calls it needs, already run; how many there are varies by round. Later rounds
build on what you established earlier and will not repeat it, so keep your own
answers.

TOOLS (results are inlined; you cannot issue new calls)
  db_lookup(table=, select=, where=, group_by=)   the measurement store
  schema_lookup(trait=)                           which modality records a trait, and in what unit
  qc_lookup(table=, select=, where=)              flags, exclusions, usable counts
  design_lookup(aspect=)                          the protocol: dose series, accession panel,
                                                  imaging rounds, replication plan
  literature_lookup(species=, property=)          published claims
  embedding_lookup(space=, query=, k=)            nearest accessions by profile similarity

TERMS
  treatment        the cadmium dose, in mg kg-1
  genotype         the accession label
  Fv/Fm_Lss        photosystem-II efficiency, from the fc1 modality
  area_mm2         leaf area
  n_plants         distinct plants behind a cell
  round_to_round   variance of an accession's per-round means
  within_round     variance between plants inside one imaging round

ROUND 1 - TASK (dose_response_shape)

RULES
